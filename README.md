# Tech & STEAM Ecosystem Spain

**An interactive guide to job portals and professional resources for technology and STEAM in Spain.**

> **Status: under development.** Content, features and links may change while the project is reviewed and expanded.

🌐 **Live site:** [gabrielagranja.github.io/ecosistema-tech-steam-espana](https://gabrielagranja.github.io/ecosistema-tech-steam-espana/) (the site content is currently in Spanish; an English version is planned).

## What you will find

- A directory of job portals with filters by experience level, work mode and specialty.
- A guidance assistant to explore search channels based on your profile.
- Salary ranges and an indicative freelance-equivalence calculator.
- A section with analysis and resources on the Tech and STEAM job market.

The goal is to help people explore options and make external sources easier to reach. The guide does not publish its own job offers and does not guarantee that any vacancy is still available.
